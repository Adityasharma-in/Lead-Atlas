Import notes

    What gets recognised

    Messy files welcome.

    Columns are matched by name, so you do not need our exact export format. Common aliases and Apify-style fields are understood automatically.

    • Business name, category and address
    • Phone numbers, emails and websites
    • Social profiles, place IDs and map links
    • Ratings, reviews, coordinates and status
    • Outreach status, contact dates and notes
    • Any extra column — kept in the drawer and exports

    Stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded, stored on a server, or sent to Apify.

    Accepted file shapes

    Lead Atlas CSV or JSON exports, Apify Google Maps dataset JSON, generic spreadsheets with headers, JSONL/NDJSON files, and JSON documents with a leads or items array.
