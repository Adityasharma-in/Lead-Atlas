Questions

Good to
know.

Still deciding? Start a scan and see the report before you save anything.

Where do my leads live?

In your own Supabase project, in tables only your signed-in account can read or write. Lead Atlas never sees another customer’s library, and row level security enforces the boundary at the database.

Do I need an Apify account?

Yes. Scans run the official Google Maps Scraper on your Apify account. Create a token in Apify Console and paste it into Lead Atlas. The app validates it, then saves it to your Supabase profile so your signed-in devices can reuse it.

Can I import a file I already have?

Yes. The Data desk accepts CSV, TSV, JSON and JSONL files up to 8 MB or 5,000 leads. It parses them in your browser, then lets you save the cleaned report to your library.

What happens to outreach marks?

They are saved with the lead in your library, so the next time you open the report the call trail is still there. They are also included in CSV and JSON exports.

Is this affiliated with Google or Apify?

No. Lead Atlas is an independent workspace built on top of Apify’s public actor. Respect Google’s terms, Apify’s terms, and applicable marketing and privacy laws.
